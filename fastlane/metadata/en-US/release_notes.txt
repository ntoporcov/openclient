More control over your conversations and a clearer view of ongoing work.

• Queue your next message or steer the assistant while it works on OpenCode v2.
• Ask a Side Question without interrupting the task or adding it to the conversation. Hold Send to choose an action.
• Group tool activity, with expandable details and animated tool counts. Customize reasoning and context visibility independently.
• Find and run your OpenCode v2 commands and skills from the / menu. Hold an entry to read its details, pin it, or share it.
• Access MCP, Git, and the workspace terminal directly from the chat header popover.
• Explore the new interactive feature announcement and revisit announcements from Help.
• Improvements to reconnecting, transcript history, provider usage setup, and plugin image display.

OpenClient supports OpenCode v1 and v2. Queue, Steer, and Side Question require v2.
